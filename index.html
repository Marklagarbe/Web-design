THE BLUE PLANET

EARTH

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place we know of that supports life. Its axis of rotation is tilted with respect to its orbital plane, producing seasons on Earth. The gravitational interaction between Earth and the Moon causes tides, stabilizes Earth's orientation on its axis, and gradually slows its rotation.
